# Privacy Policy — Hisn Al-Muslim | Islamic Companion

**Last updated: September 18, 2026**

Hisn Al-Muslim | Islamic Companion ("the App") is an Islamic companion application designed to provide Quran content, adhkar and supplications, prayer times, tasbeeh, Islamic calendar features, audio content, and other Islamic resources.

This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information when you use it.

## 1. Information We Collect

We aim to keep data collection to a minimum.

The App does not require you to create an account, and we do not intentionally collect personal information such as your name, phone number, address, or passwords through the App.

Some features may request access to device services or permissions, as described below.

## 2. Location Information

The App may request access to your device's approximate or precise location to calculate or provide accurate prayer times for your current location.

Location information is used for the relevant feature and is not intentionally stored as a personal profile or sold to third parties.

If location access is not granted, you may still be able to use the App by selecting a location manually, where supported.

## 3. Notifications and Alarms

The App may request permission to send notifications or schedule alarms/reminders for supported Islamic features, such as prayer reminders.

Notification permissions can be controlled or revoked through your device settings.

## 4. Audio and Media

The App may provide Quran recitations and other Islamic audio content. Audio playback may use locally bundled resources or third-party content/services depending on the feature and version of the App.

The App does not use microphone access to record conversations or personal audio as part of its normal Islamic features.

## 5. Device Permissions

Depending on the features available in your installed version, the App may request permissions related to:

- Location — to calculate prayer times.
- Notifications — to provide reminders and scheduled notifications.
- Network/Internet access — to retrieve supported online content or services.
- Audio/media playback — to play Quran and other supported audio content.

Permissions are requested only when needed by the corresponding feature.

## 6. Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services or libraries to provide certain technical or content-related functionality.

Such services may process technical information according to their own privacy policies. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Where applicable, third-party services may include services used for application infrastructure, online content, crash reporting, analytics, or media delivery.

## 7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through the App. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 8. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us so that we can review the situation.

## 9. Data Retention and Deletion

Because the App does not require a user account and does not intentionally maintain a personal profile, there is generally no user account data to delete.

Some information handled by third-party services, if applicable, may be retained according to those services' own policies.

## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the App, its features, or applicable requirements.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
